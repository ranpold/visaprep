Tell us your route and dates and we'll find real scheduled flights, or enter them yourself. Then add your stays and daily plans. Your itinerary PDF is marked as a plan, not a ticket, and shows the real status of each item. That's what most consulates ask for. Drafts save automatically in your browser.

Don't buy non-refundable tickets before your visa is approved. If your consulate insists on a verifiable reservation, use a free 24-hour hold or a refundable fare, then set that flight's status to "Held" or "Booked".
