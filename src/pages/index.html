Why not buy a "dummy ticket"?

Paid dummy-ticket sites charge $5–$25 for a PDF that looks like an airline ticket. Visa officers see these every day. A document that pretends to be a booking but isn't one can count as misrepresentation, and that can mean refusal or even a ban. Most consulates ask for a travel plan or reservation, not a paid ticket. An honest plan costs nothing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a flight ticket to apply for a visa?

Usually not. Schengen consulates, the UK, and the US all advise against buying non-refundable tickets before your visa is approved. Most ask for a travel itinerary or a reservation. Read our flight itinerary guide for details.

Is the itinerary PDF a flight ticket?

No, and it doesn't pretend to be. It's clearly marked as a planned itinerary. That's the honest way to show your travel plans, and it's what many consulates ask for.

What if the embassy requires a verifiable reservation?

Some do. Many airlines let you hold a fare, or cancel for free within 24 hours. Hotels with free cancellation work the same way. See the free flight hold guide.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything you type stays in your browser. We have no accounts and no database. Your drafts are saved in your browser's local storage so you can come back to them.
